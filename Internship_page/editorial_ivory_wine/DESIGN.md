## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intellectual rigor of classic academia fused with the precision of contemporary Bangalore technology culture. It moves consciously away from ubiquitous silicon-valley neon glows, synthetic gradients, and sterile blue-gray dashboards. Instead, it operates with the visual weight, tactility, and poise of an archival academic monograph.

The target audience comprises ambitious learners, career-pivoting engineers, academic fellows, and hiring directors seeking substance over hype. The emotional resonance is grounded, quiet, and deeply credible—fostering confidence through spatial generosity, refined proportions, and tactile paper-like warmth.

### Visual Pillars
- **Curated Print Heritage:** Layouts take cues from literary broadsheets and university presses, utilizing delicate hairline framing, distinct editorial mastheads, and classic asymmetrical margins.
- **Architectural Grounding:** Heavy deep-wine surfaces anchor major focal points, counterbalanced by generous breathing room rendered in ivory tones.
- **Crafted Restraint:** Eliminates synthetic glassmorphism, floating drop-shadow layers, and artificial glowing borders. Elevation is achieved via tonal contrast and fine graphic rules.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic neutrality with deep vinous authority. Color application is deliberate and disciplined; color is used to punctuate hierarchy rather than decorate empty space.

### Color Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#F8F3EC`):** Warm unbleached ivory that softens optical fatigue and evokes high-grade bookstock.
- **Deep Burgundy (`#5A0B2E`):** The primary brand anchor. Used for primary interactive actions, key category badges, and authoritative accents.
- **Dark Burgundy (`#3B071F`):** Anchors high-impact structural blocks, footers, narrative spotlight banners, and inverted editorial modules.
- **Deep Text Near-Black (`#171417`):** Delivers uncompromised contrast against ivory and crisp readability across all display scales.
- **Secondary Text Warm Gray (`#6B6464`):** Preserves visual hierarchy for metadata, footnotes, secondary descriptions, and captions.
- **Subtle Warm Beige Border (`#DED5CC`):** Used for 1px hairline rules, module dividers, and crisp card strokes.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated focal containers, input surfaces, and card interiors to preserve crisp definition against the ivory base.

## Typography

Typography establishes the dual identity of the design system: intellectual prestige conveyed through `Playfair Display`, counterweighted by utilitarian clarity rendered via `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Typographic Rules
- **Headline Architecture:** `Playfair Display` is strictly reserved for narrative framing, section headers, thematic titles, and lead pull quotes. It maintains tight line tracking and comfortable line heights to preserve editorial grace.
- **Informational Precision:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` governs course curriculum matrices, technical documentation, operational UI elements, tabular data, and transactional forms.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Small labels and metadata keys employ upper-case typesetting with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic book folio tags and archival stamps.
- **Editorial Balance:** Never stack two sans-serif headers of similar sizes. Emphasize contrast: a large serif headline followed directly by a quiet, measured sans-serif lead paragraph.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on a structured 12-column grid with deliberate asymmetrical breathing room. Layouts evoke print publishing through crisp boundaries, generous section padding, and purposeful rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of `1280px`. Gutters scale at `2rem` (`32px`), with lateral page margins of `2.5rem` to `4rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Gutters set to `1.5rem` (`24px`). Lateral padding set to `2rem`.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins and `1rem` gutters. Content blocks collapse into single-column vertical stacks.

### Rhythmic Guidance
- Section transitions must have substantial vertical presence (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) on desktop views to prevent visual clutter and signal calm authority.
- Horizontal dividers should be rendered as continuous 1px rules colored `#DED5CC`, running either edge-to-edge or scoped cleanly to module widths.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, colored halos, and blurry glow fields. Elevation is expressed via architectural planes, hairline borders, and subtle contrast changes between `#F8F3EC` and `#FFFFFF`.

### Hierarchy Through Planar Layering
- **Base Level (Canvas):** The primary ivory surface (`#F8F3EC`). All foundational reading and narrative structures rest directly here.
- **Card & Cell Level:** Elevated content blocks use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces framed by a crisp 1px border (`#DED5CC`). No box-shadow is necessary in default static states.
- **Interactive Hover State:** Elements gain subtle physical presence using an extremely soft, warm-tinted shadow (`0 4px 16px rgba(59, 7, 31, 0.05)`), combined with a 1px border shift to primary wine (`#5A0B2E`).
- **Recessed / Embedded Modules:** Applied for curriculum details, code examples, or side notes using a subtly darkened neutral ivory (`#EFE8DE`) with an inset border structure.
- **Inverted Focus Anchors:** Critical institutional calls-to-action and thematic narrative features shift completely into deep burgundy (`#3B071F`), creating high-contrast depth across full horizontal viewport bands.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes architectural precision and timeless discipline. Roundedness is intentionally restrained (`0.25rem` base) to reflect tailored paper trim and crafted print modules rather than playful consumer app geometry.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro UI Elements (Pills, small tags, badges):** `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`) or occasionally full pill (`9999px`) only when indicating status indicators or categorical tags.
- **Standard UI Elements (Buttons, inputs, dropdown items):** `0.25rem` (`4px`). Retains sharp, disciplined edges.
- **Cards, Containers, & Dialogue Sheets:** `0.5rem` (`8px` / `rounded-lg`). Provides subtle softness without breaking structural clarity.
- **Strict Prohibition:** Never use oversized bubbly curves (e.g., `16px`–`24px` corner radii) on content cards or data tables, as they undermine academic prestige.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#5A0B2E` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, 1px `#5A0B2E` border. High-touch hover transitions smoothly to `#3B071F`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent background, 1px `#5A0B2E` border, `#5A0B2E` text. On hover: shifts to subtle `#5A0B2E` tint at 6% opacity.
- **Editorial Ghost:** Clean text link paired with an arrow icon (`→`), featuring an underlined rule that thickens subtly on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Category Chips:** Ivory or white surface, 1px `#DED5CC` border, `#5A0B2E` label text, `label-sm` typographic styling with uppercase tracking.
- **Active / Filtered Chips:** Solid `#5A0B2E` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards & Curriculum Panels
- **Standard Content Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, framed by 1px `#DED5CC` border, `0.5rem` radius, and generous internal padding (`space-xl`).
- **Feature Card:** Dual-tone construction featuring a subtle header divider rule (`#DED5CC`) separating the title and metadata from syllabus content.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Fields:** White background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px `#DED5CC` border, `0.25rem` border radius, `#171417` value text, and `#6B6464` placeholder text.
- **Focus State:** 1px border transitions crisp to `#5A0B2E` with an subtle outline ring of `2px rgba(90, 11, 46, 0.12)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Unchecked:** Flat white square or circle with a 1.5px `#DED5CC` stroke.
- **Checked:** Solid `#5A0B2E` fill with an ivory tick or center dot.

### Academic & Tech Modules
- **Editorial Masthead Block:** Centered or asymmetric split layout featuring deep Playfair headline, tracking metadata line, and a single horizontal hairline rule.
- **Faculty / Mentor Credential Pill:** Compact white card featuring a subtle monochrome or warm-graded portrait, serif name, and monospace/sans credential tags.
